# Week 01: Orientation & Introduction to Data Science

## 1. Overview & Key Themes
- **Core Definition:** Data science is not just crunching numbers; it is about solving real-world problems and creating actionable business value through scientific experimentation.
- **Data Matters When It Drives Action:** More data alone does not guarantee insight. Data must inform practical decisions (e.g., inventory, scheduling, churn reduction).
- **The Scientific Loop in Data Science:**
  1. *Hypothesize:* Define a clear question/prediction before touching the code.
  2. *Experiment:* Gather fair comparisons and prepare the data.
  3. *Analyze:* Measure results against the initial hypothesis.
  4. *Revise:* Adapt the model/strategy based on concrete evidence.
- **The Modern Role of AI:** Tools accelerate execution, but formulating the problem, building hypotheses, and validating results remain human responsibilities.

---

## 2. The CRISP-DM Framework
- **Business Understanding:** Translate high-level goals into measurable metrics (KPIs/KGIs).
- **Data Understanding & Dark Data:** Recognize what data is available versus what critical information might be hidden or missing.
- **Data Preparation & Modeling:** Cleaning, preprocessing, and building statistical/ML models.
- **Evaluation & Expansion:** Measuring against business impact before deployment.
